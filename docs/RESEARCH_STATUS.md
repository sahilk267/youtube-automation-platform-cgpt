# Competitor and 120-channel research — evidence quality

`research/` contains the 2026-11 desk-research pack: competitor workflows, 12 × 10 channel benchmark shortlist (120 total), architecture decision and proposed 600-video sampling protocol. **Not** a real-time top-ten subscriber ranking; **not** frame-by-frame viewing of 120 complete catalogs; **not** access to private creator analytics/RPM/retention. Treat qualitative descriptions as hypotheses to validate with actual owned/authorized evidence.

Competitor roles: InVideo/Pictory script-to-scenes, Descript transcript editing, OpusClip contextual clip extraction, HeyGen dubbing, vidIQ/1of10/Viewstats discovery and outlier analysis, ComfyUI reusable GPU workflows, n8n scheduling. Providers' own advertised features are not independent performance benchmarks.

Each domain/channel benchmark should eventually contain dated sample video IDs, screenshots or authorized clips (only where licensed), specific observed hook/narrative/visual/audio/rights features, sources and reproducible score rubric. Use the protocol described in `research/.../04_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_VALIDATION_PLAN.md`. Do not use channel examples to copy identity, third-party footage or copyrighted presentation wholesale.

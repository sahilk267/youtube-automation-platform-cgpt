# ViralOS — 120-Channel Content Benchmark Dossier

**Research date:** 9 November 2026. **Scope:** 12 domain OS × 10 editorially selected **benchmark channels**. **NOT an exact top-ten subscriber ranking.** Channel names/styles are drawn from 2026 creator guides, public channel descriptions and selected primary-source examples. Do not infer that every video on each channel was watched or timed. These are individual **format/production hypotheses**, not private analytics or measured causal retention findings.

## Selection method and evidence grades

Channels selected to cover distinct content production approaches: reporting/experiment/demo/animation/commentary/data/tactical/educational, as well as India/US relevance and reuse constraints. A = direct creator/channel/league/source artifact corroborates concrete format; B = 2026 independent creator guide or public channel description; C = plausible format characterization needing direct video-level sampling. These entries are typically B; some have A anchors. No invented subscriber/revenue/retention numbers, no creator private data.

### How to turn this into a genuinely deep VIDEO-level audit

For **each** of 120 channels, sample at least five actual videos before claiming full media analysis: newest long-form, newest Short, most-viewed evergreen, recent breakout normalized against channel baseline, and a typical non-outlier. Record permanent URL, upload timestamp, title, thumbnail, runtime, first-30-second hook beats, first payoff, scene changes, actual on-screen examples, graphic/caption layers, narration/SFX characteristics, evidence/rights, language, clear limitations, and timestamps. Use public views only as public metadata; never fabricate CTR/retention/RPM. This turn has not performed that 600-video viewing protocol.

# CORE-17 — EVIDENCE, SOURCE COVERAGE, DOMAIN RESEARCH STOP RULES

1. Source class: PRIMARY_RECORD, FIRST_HAND_REPORT, INDEPENDENT_REPORT, SYNDICATED_REPRINT, OPINION, USER_COMMENT, SATIRE, UNKNOWN. Copying a source across 20 websites adds **zero** independent confirmations.
2. Each factual assertion in an approved script must reference its span in `SourceVersion` or reproducible measurement, with date, speaker/subject disambiguation, quote language and time context. `support`, `refute`, `context`, and `correction` are separate relations; absence of evidence is not proof of negation.
3. A domain research plan has explicit finite questions, query variants, time range, allowed providers, max external calls, wall-time budget, and evidence sufficient criteria. No research call claims `ALL_SOURCES_FOUND` or `ALL_SPEECHES_FOUND`.
4. Each attempt yields `SourceCoverageReport`: provider, query, timestamp, return count, outage/quota/missing-authorization status, uncertainty, unanswered questions, reviewer flags. A provider failure can never be silently mapped to an empty result set.
5. `RESEARCH_SUFFICIENT_FOR_DRAFT` means enough to draft with caveats, not factual infallibility. Publish requires source-specific high-impact verification; sensitive disputed claims need independent corroboration + human/editorial review, proportionate treatment, legal/risk steps.
6. Historical contradiction requires comparable propositions: same speaker/entity, same subject, scope, timeframe, quantifier, conditional assumptions and unit. Changes in policy or new circumstances are not proof of dishonesty. Record counterexamples and contextual interpretations.
7. Detect source change by ETag/Last-Modified where supported and content hash always; new version invalidates dependent claim reviews only where passages changed; analysis may be stale until verified. Cache TTL depends on match status and source policy; no one default TTL for live score and historical record. Re-check prior to time-sensitive publication.
8. Source permission tiers: metadata-only if terms disallow full archival copy; maintain stable link/hash and local excerpt limits. Media rights separate from research source rights. Protect identities of confidential sources, and do not use unauthorized access or misrepresent first-hand interviews.
9. Classify statements as verified fact, disputed allegation, opinion, prediction, satire, or uncertainty. Viewer likes/comments, score trends or LLM consensus do not count as verification.

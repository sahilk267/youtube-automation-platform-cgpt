# CORE-19 — YOUTUBE-FIRST EDITORIAL PORTFOLIO, GROWTH AND GPU FUND

- Primary business: **owner's YouTube channels only** until separate signed decision. Never add paid client sales, subscription checkout or SaaS production tasks to Launch.
- Select niches using measured own-channel data and source-rights feasibility; India cricket and USA NFL are hypotheses, not guaranteed top niches or earnings. Baseball/NBA/tech/news/science backlogs remain documented for eventual enablement.
- Per-market channel identity: India cricket Hindi/Hinglish; US NFL English initially proposed, stored as editable owner profiles. Keep league/time zone rules explicit.
- Shorts for experiment/discovery and long-form for retention/watch-hours; compare actual cohorts by impression source, age of video, audience segment and publication window. Do not claim causal improvement from small observational samples.
- Reused video policy and copyright are distinct gates. Green-screen appearance alone does not establish substantive transformation; no presumed fair use, no copyrighted highlight repost automation, no fake views/likes/comments.
- Each analytics metric has `platform`, `observed_at`, `window`, `availability`, `sample`, `channel_id`, `video_id` and authorization provenance. Unavailable metrics display `N/A`, not zero. Predictions are stored in separate scenario namespace.
- Revenue ledger stores currency minor units, real YouTube payments and verified/owner-entered actuals separately from estimates. GPU savings target user-entered only, and hardware purchase budget includes PSU/storage/cooling/other dependencies if known.
- Publish opportunities may be rejected for insufficient originality/value, even if trending. Viewer benefit and factual correctness are hard quality constraints, not a weight outweighed by viral score.
- Campaign calendar considers sports fixtures, off-season evergreen plans, corrections, news sensitivity and local-vs-US time zones. No forced daily volume.

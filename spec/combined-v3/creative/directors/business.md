# BUSINESS — Expert Creative Director v3.0

**Binding:** `domain_id=business`; implement as **policy + specialist domain algorithms**, never a duplicate renderer. `core/21_EVIDENCE_TO_SCREEN_V3.md` and strict `contracts/v3` govern shared artifacts. **Status: SPECIFIED, NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

## Audience and unique creative objective
Viewers learning corporate strategy and economics from real filings, not investment promises.

## Source/evidence prerequisites
Audited filings, period-specific financials, earnings transcripts, ownership records and explicit currency conversions.

## Honest opening (not boilerplate)
A precise business-model or financial mystery supported by a dated fact.

## Story sequence / exact decision policy
1. Explain how product creates revenue.
2. Trace period-specific financial flow using sourced values.
3. Compare unit economics and competing company choices.
4. Stress-test assumptions and show best counterexample.
5. Summarize robust inference versus speculation.

## Approved screen grammar
- Original labeled revenue flow/waterfall
- Dated 10-K/annual report source card with accounting period
- Peer comparison with currency and inflation context
- Ownership graph with dated primary links

## Voice/audio policy
- Analytical, patient, define gross versus net and financial terms
- Financial arithmetic read with units and fiscal year

## Text, data and overlay rules
- Audited / unaudited / estimate flags
- Dollars/rupees, scales, fiscal period and exchange-rate source

## Allowed narrative formats (requires format-pack validation)
`CASE_STUDY`, `LONG_DOCUMENTARY`, `SHORT_EXPLAINER`, `BREAKING_BRIEF`

## Payoff and ending
Explain uncertainty and factors to monitor rather than guarantee stock direction.

## Mandatory exception and accuracy gates
- No unsupported fraud allegations or investment advice
- Visually large bars must not hide changes of axis or period

## Local compute mode
**CPU without dedicated GPU:** Data charts, balance-sheet illustrations, original business diagrams.
**Optional future GPU:** Optional conceptual business environments only as illustrations, not evidence of actual company locations.

## Golden fixture and independent manual acceptance
Reconcile revenue units/period, missing subsidiaries and footnote context.

A human reviewer must inspect actual rendered Short and long-form from this director for factual/rights accuracy, sound, screen readability, novelty, pacing, start/end payoff. Tests must show a difference from other category outputs **beyond swapped fonts/colors/voice**. Record inputs and source URLs; do not invent viewer retention.

# CAREER — Expert Creative Director v3.0

**Binding:** `domain_id=career`; implement as **policy + specialist domain algorithms**, never a duplicate renderer. `core/21_EVIDENCE_TO_SCREEN_V3.md` and strict `contracts/v3` govern shared artifacts. **Status: SPECIFIED, NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

## Audience and unique creative objective
Jobseekers and students choosing skills, roles or interview strategies in specific labor markets.

## Source/evidence prerequisites
Dated regional job postings/stats, employers, credential recognition, actual verified salary studies and disclosed limitations.

## Honest opening (not boilerplate)
A precise actionable career question tied to region, role and seniority.

## Story sequence / exact decision policy
1. Clarify role/market and audience experience level.
2. Show evidence for demand and skills with dates.
3. Demonstrate one concrete resume/project/interview tactic.
4. Show tradeoffs and risks of the pathway.
5. End with achievable next action and checking resources.

## Approved screen grammar
- Source-linked skill demand matrix
- Original roadmap graph, interview structure and portfolio sample
- Dated salary distributions with sample-method note
- Checklist progression without guaranteed job outcomes

## Voice/audio policy
- Supportive and specific, not exaggerated income rhetoric
- Pronounce credentials and region-specific terms accurately

## Text, data and overlay rules
- Country, sample size, year, currency and assumptions
- Action step with measurable outcome, no fake testimonials

## Allowed narrative formats (requires format-pack validation)
`STEP_LESSON`, `SHORT_EXPLAINER`, `CASE_STUDY`, `DEMO_REVIEW`

## Payoff and ending
Concrete next step and responsible uncertainty about hiring.

## Mandatory exception and accuracy gates
- Do not invent salary ranges or guarantee jobs
- Personal job applications, documents and identifiable employer data protected

## Local compute mode
**CPU without dedicated GPU:** Roadmaps, original resume demos, portfolio walkthroughs.
**Optional future GPU:** Optional polished backgrounds but primary value is specific guidance.

## Golden fixture and independent manual acceptance
Block unsourced salary claim or fake certification requirement.

A human reviewer must inspect actual rendered Short and long-form from this director for factual/rights accuracy, sound, screen readability, novelty, pacing, start/end payoff. Tests must show a difference from other category outputs **beyond swapped fonts/colors/voice**. Record inputs and source URLs; do not invent viewer retention.

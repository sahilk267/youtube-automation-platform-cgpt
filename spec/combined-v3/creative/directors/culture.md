# CULTURE — Expert Creative Director v3.0

**Binding:** `domain_id=culture`; implement as **policy + specialist domain algorithms**, never a duplicate renderer. `core/21_EVIDENCE_TO_SCREEN_V3.md` and strict `contracts/v3` govern shared artifacts. **Status: SPECIFIED, NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

## Audience and unique creative objective
Online community viewers curious about provenance, cultural meaning and the real origins of viral phenomena.

## Source/evidence prerequisites
Earliest available attributed posts and archives, independent timeline, creator interviews/claims with provenance, context and permissions.

## Honest opening (not boilerplate)
Identify a real origin question rather than mock an identifiable person.

## Story sequence / exact decision policy
1. Show attributed trend reference without assuming ownership.
2. Trace posts/platform/region and differing timelines.
3. Explain how format was modified and why viewers noticed it.
4. Evaluate claims and social context, opposing interpretations.
5. Provide a nuanced provenance-aware takeaway.

## Approved screen grammar
- Timeline/map of public post diffusion using sourced dates
- Attributed examples and original reenacted diagrams where rights are unclear
- Caption cards distinguishing rumor and verified origin
- Context comparisons with creator permissions where required

## Voice/audio policy
- Contextual conversational tone, avoid harassment or group stereotypes
- Respect cultural pronunciation and explicit slang caveats

## Text, data and overlay rules
- Alleged first post / oldest verified record distinctions
- Platform/date/original credit and uncertainty status

## Allowed narrative formats (requires format-pack validation)
`SHORT_EXPLAINER`, `LONG_DOCUMENTARY`, `REACTION_REVIEW`, `CASE_STUDY`

## Payoff and ending
Best-established origin plus uncertainty and respectful attribution.

## Mandatory exception and accuracy gates
- Harassment, privacy, creator rights, cultural stereotyping and source deletion
- Remix popularity is not evidence of who originated a meme

## Local compute mode
**CPU without dedicated GPU:** Original timelines, text cards, ethically cleared snippets and annotations.
**Optional future GPU:** Labeled fictional illustrative background; never forge original viral clips.

## Golden fixture and independent manual acceptance
Reject false earliest-source claim and unauthorized personal clip.

A human reviewer must inspect actual rendered Short and long-form from this director for factual/rights accuracy, sound, screen readability, novelty, pacing, start/end payoff. Tests must show a difference from other category outputs **beyond swapped fonts/colors/voice**. Record inputs and source URLs; do not invent viewer retention.

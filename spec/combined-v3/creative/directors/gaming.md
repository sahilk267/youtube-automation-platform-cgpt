# GAMING — Expert Creative Director v3.0

**Binding:** `domain_id=gaming`; implement as **policy + specialist domain algorithms**, never a duplicate renderer. `core/21_EVIDENCE_TO_SCREEN_V3.md` and strict `contracts/v3` govern shared artifacts. **Status: SPECIFIED, NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

## Audience and unique creative objective
Fans seeking genuine gameplay progress, entertainment, mechanical insights or actionable strategy.

## Source/evidence prerequisites
Owner-approved game capture/replays, publisher policy, patch notes, game version, actual input and observable outcomes.

## Honest opening (not boilerplate)
Show authentic meaningful gameplay challenge or a surprising strategy with exact patch/version context.

## Story sequence / exact decision policy
1. Name genuine challenge and measurable condition.
2. Show original attempt including setbacks and reactions.
3. Pause to explain surprising mechanics with a diagram.
4. Show actual consequence of applying the strategy.
5. Pay off the challenge and next authentic goal.

## Approved screen grammar
- Original unmodified gameplay excerpts with timestamped capture manifest
- HUD, map arrows, controls and hitbox diagrams
- Before-after actual rounds, not staged AI footage
- Patch note quotations and version tags

## Voice/audio policy
- Excitement appropriate to gameplay, no forced artificial shouting
- Game SFX/music require publisher/piece-specific permission; narration remains clear

## Text, data and overlay rules
- Game title and patch/version
- Objective/status counters, genuine outcomes and spoiler gates

## Allowed narrative formats (requires format-pack validation)
`GAMEPLAY_ANALYSIS`, `SHORT_EXPLAINER`, `STEP_LESSON`, `REACTION_REVIEW`

## Payoff and ending
Real result, explain strategy and disclose any edit that compresses attempts.

## Mandatory exception and accuracy gates
- Do not pretend AI-generated gameplay is authentic
- Publisher music/content licensing and streamer clips differ

## Local compute mode
**CPU without dedicated GPU:** Real capture and light annotations with honest time-compression labels.
**Optional future GPU:** GPU can accelerate editing, not fabricate achievements.

## Golden fixture and independent manual acceptance
Version mismatch, fake win overlay, unauthorized streamer clip should fail.

A human reviewer must inspect actual rendered Short and long-form from this director for factual/rights accuracy, sound, screen readability, novelty, pacing, start/end payoff. Tests must show a difference from other category outputs **beyond swapped fonts/colors/voice**. Record inputs and source URLs; do not invent viewer retention.

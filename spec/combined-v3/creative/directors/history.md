# HISTORY — Expert Creative Director v3.0

**Binding:** `domain_id=history`; implement as **policy + specialist domain algorithms**, never a duplicate renderer. `core/21_EVIDENCE_TO_SCREEN_V3.md` and strict `contracts/v3` govern shared artifacts. **Status: SPECIFIED, NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

## Audience and unique creative objective
People seeking clear chronological reconstruction, provenance and competing scholarly interpretations.

## Source/evidence prerequisites
Primary archives, academic secondary works, date conversions, translation provenance and historical uncertainties.

## Honest opening (not boilerplate)
A meaningful historical question rooted in an actual artifact rather than sensational myth.

## Story sequence / exact decision policy
1. Situate era and people with dated records.
2. Show primary artifact and interpret its origin/bias.
3. Build chronological sequence with period geography.
4. Present strongest alternative historian account.
5. Conclude what is evidenced and still debated.

## Approved screen grammar
- Original animated historical border maps with period accuracy
- Legible source-document closeups and citation
- Chronology strips and geography of campaigns/events
- Clearly labeled artistic reconstructions separate from photos

## Voice/audio policy
- Narrative but careful; pronounce names and transliteration accurately
- Respectful restrained music that does not sensationalize tragedy

## Text, data and overlay rules
- Date system, region name changes, source locator
- RECONSTRUCTION / ARCHIVAL / UNCERTAIN overlays

## Allowed narrative formats (requires format-pack validation)
`LONG_DOCUMENTARY`, `SHORT_EXPLAINER`, `CASE_STUDY`, `STEP_LESSON`

## Payoff and ending
Sourced historical consensus, dispute and further documented question.

## Mandatory exception and accuracy gates
- Avoid modern borders in old era without disclaimer
- AI-generated historical faces must not be passed off as authentic photographs

## Local compute mode
**CPU without dedicated GPU:** Custom period maps, timelines, archival still crops with permissions.
**Optional future GPU:** Labeled illustrative environment reconstruction with continuity bible.

## Golden fixture and independent manual acceptance
Catch wrong-calendar year, anachronistic objects, period maps and mistranslated source.

A human reviewer must inspect actual rendered Short and long-form from this director for factual/rights accuracy, sound, screen readability, novelty, pacing, start/end payoff. Tests must show a difference from other category outputs **beyond swapped fonts/colors/voice**. Record inputs and source URLs; do not invent viewer retention.

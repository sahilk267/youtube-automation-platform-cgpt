# LEARNING — Expert Creative Director v3.0

**Binding:** `domain_id=learning`; implement as **policy + specialist domain algorithms**, never a duplicate renderer. `core/21_EVIDENCE_TO_SCREEN_V3.md` and strict `contracts/v3` govern shared artifacts. **Status: SPECIFIED, NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

## Audience and unique creative objective
Learners with prerequisites and misconceptions; optimize comprehension and achievement, not only retention.

## Source/evidence prerequisites
Accepted syllabus, validated teaching examples, solved problems and subject-specific reference material.

## Honest opening (not boilerplate)
Tell learner what they will be able to solve or explain by the end.

## Story sequence / exact decision policy
1. Ask diagnostic question and surface prerequisite.
2. Teach one concept using worked example.
3. Reveal common misconception with comparison.
4. Give guided practice and answer reasoning.
5. Summarize skill and next step.

## Approved screen grammar
- Whiteboard with readable handwritten-style vector steps
- Highlight exact transformation in equation/diagram
- Progressive problem solving without changing notation
- Brief pause/quiz prompt that actually tests recall

## Voice/audio policy
- Patient instructional tone and deliberate explanation of step changes
- Short silence can allow learner attempt; avoid frantic cuts

## Text, data and overlay rules
- Terms/formulas visible long enough to parse
- Practice prompt, answer justification and difficulty level

## Allowed narrative formats (requires format-pack validation)
`STEP_LESSON`, `LONG_DOCUMENTARY`, `SHORT_EXPLAINER`, `DEMO_REVIEW`

## Payoff and ending
Worked answer and measurable learning goal, not filler CTA.

## Mandatory exception and accuracy gates
- Answer correctness, accessibility and language-dependent prerequisite terminology
- Do not use copyrighted textbooks wholesale

## Local compute mode
**CPU without dedicated GPU:** Original board animation, plotting, progressively revealed diagrams.
**Optional future GPU:** Optional illustrations only where pedagogically useful; not artificial demonstrations.

## Golden fixture and independent manual acceptance
Misconception, incorrect solution step or unreadable equation blocks release.

A human reviewer must inspect actual rendered Short and long-form from this director for factual/rights accuracy, sound, screen readability, novelty, pacing, start/end payoff. Tests must show a difference from other category outputs **beyond swapped fonts/colors/voice**. Record inputs and source URLs; do not invent viewer retention.

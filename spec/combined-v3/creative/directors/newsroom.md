# NEWSROOM — Expert Creative Director v3.0

**Binding:** `domain_id=newsroom`; implement as **policy + specialist domain algorithms**, never a duplicate renderer. `core/21_EVIDENCE_TO_SCREEN_V3.md` and strict `contracts/v3` govern shared artifacts. **Status: SPECIFIED, NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

## Audience and unique creative objective
Public-interest viewers seeking verifiable explanations rather than an outrage montage; context and accuracy outrank editing speed.

## Source/evidence prerequisites
Full original speech/video, timecoded transcript, date-scoped historical speech, laws/official records, counterevidence and right-of-reply state.

## Honest opening (not boilerplate)
A carefully attributed public-interest question or source-backed discrepancy; no predetermined conclusion or insinuated guilt.

## Story sequence / exact decision policy
1. Provide original full-context statement and date.
2. Extract atomic claim, explain temporal scope.
3. Compare earlier statements, documented policies/actions and actual outcomes.
4. Show strongest verified contradictory/contextual evidence and reasonable alternatives.
5. Conclude proportionately with remaining unknowns, response requests and sources.

## Approved screen grammar
- Document-first original quote cards with accurate timestamps
- Side-by-side historical statement comparison explicitly matched by proposition
- Date-accurate policy/outcome chart with uncertainty
- Ownership/relationship graph only with documented dated edges

## Voice/audio policy
- Measured, precise source attribution; no impersonation of public figures
- Quiet source document holds and restrained music; leave enough time to read

## Text, data and overlay rules
- VERIFIED / ALLEGATION / OPINION / UNRESOLVED labels
- Source title, publisher, document date, passage locator and correction notice

## Allowed narrative formats (requires format-pack validation)
`BREAKING_BRIEF`, `LONG_DOCUMENTARY`, `CASE_STUDY`, `REACTION_REVIEW`, `SHORT_EXPLAINER`

## Payoff and ending
State what is established, what is disputed, who was asked to respond, and correction path.

## Mandatory exception and accuracy gates
- Defamation, privacy, misleading clip edits, speech translation and synthetic-media confusion
- Public footage is not public-domain merely because it is news

## Local compute mode
**CPU without dedicated GPU:** Readable primary-document crops, scrolling official timelines, original maps/charts and source-led narration.
**Optional future GPU:** AI reenactments only labeled as reconstructions; never present synthetic politician footage as actual evidence.

## Golden fixture and independent manual acceptance
Pair apparent contradictory statements where context changed; require class UPDATED_POSITION not CONFIRMED_CONTRADICTION unless proposition/time match.

A human reviewer must inspect actual rendered Short and long-form from this director for factual/rights accuracy, sound, screen readability, novelty, pacing, start/end payoff. Tests must show a difference from other category outputs **beyond swapped fonts/colors/voice**. Record inputs and source URLs; do not invent viewer retention.

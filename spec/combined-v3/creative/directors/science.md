# SCIENCE — Expert Creative Director v3.0

**Binding:** `domain_id=science`; implement as **policy + specialist domain algorithms**, never a duplicate renderer. `core/21_EVIDENCE_TO_SCREEN_V3.md` and strict `contracts/v3` govern shared artifacts. **Status: SPECIFIED, NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

## Audience and unique creative objective
Curious learners who need mechanisms, evidence strength, study limitations and accurate takeaways.

## Source/evidence prerequisites
Primary papers with DOI/version, methods, sample and statistical context; corroboration/replication, retractions and consensus status.

## Honest opening (not boilerplate)
Genuine observable phenomenon or surprising finding qualified to actual evidence, not extrapolating one study to universal proof.

## Story sequence / exact decision policy
1. Show phenomenon and question.
2. Explain physical/biological mechanism via original spatial diagram.
3. Show how researchers measured the effect, units and design limitations.
4. Compare alternative explanations or replication.
5. Give honest conclusion and open research.

## Approved screen grammar
- Mechanism animation labeling observation versus hypothesis
- Axis- and unit-labeled original plot from reported values
- Study population/experiment design diagram
- Evidence-strength and uncertainty strip

## Voice/audio policy
- Clear approachable explanation of jargon and unit pronunciation
- Measured pacing for mechanisms and equations; avoid exaggerated cinematic certainty

## Text, data and overlay rules
- Simulation / observation / hypothesis clear labels
- Paper year, figure attribution, measurement unit and uncertainty note

## Allowed narrative formats (requires format-pack validation)
`SHORT_EXPLAINER`, `LONG_DOCUMENTARY`, `STEP_LESSON`, `CASE_STUDY`

## Payoff and ending
Explain what the finding means, limits on extrapolation and next test.

## Mandatory exception and accuracy gates
- No invented scientific demonstration or medical advice
- Paper figures and photographs require rights even if the paper is accessible

## Local compute mode
**CPU without dedicated GPU:** Vector experiment setup, animated pathways, narrated plots and original diagrams.
**Optional future GPU:** Possible labeled illustrative particles/organisms after science factual review, not synthetic observational data.

## Golden fixture and independent manual acceptance
Catch correlation→causation leap, mislabeled log axis and retracted-paper citation.

A human reviewer must inspect actual rendered Short and long-form from this director for factual/rights accuracy, sound, screen readability, novelty, pacing, start/end payoff. Tests must show a difference from other category outputs **beyond swapped fonts/colors/voice**. Record inputs and source URLs; do not invent viewer retention.

# SPACE — Expert Creative Director v3.0

**Binding:** `domain_id=space`; implement as **policy + specialist domain algorithms**, never a duplicate renderer. `core/21_EVIDENCE_TO_SCREEN_V3.md` and strict `contracts/v3` govern shared artifacts. **Status: SPECIFIED, NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

## Audience and unique creative objective
Astronomy viewers looking for mission timelines, scientific observations and meaningful scales.

## Source/evidence prerequisites
Mission agency public updates, observation data with timestamp/instrument, published paper, unit-correct orbital calculations.

## Honest opening (not boilerplate)
A verified new observation or thought-provoking scale question without false “NASA confirms aliens” framing.

## Story sequence / exact decision policy
1. Establish observed mission/phenomenon and its evidence source.
2. Explain telescope/sensor or measurement.
3. Show spatial/scale diagram and physics model.
4. Compare simulation with real observation.
5. Reveal what is certain and unresolved.

## Approved screen grammar
- Distance- and time-labeled orbit animation
- Agency image attribution with genuine instrument identification
- Simulation vs actual observation explicit split screen
- Scale-accurate astronomical comparisons where calculable

## Voice/audio policy
- Curious explanatory tone, correct numerical/scientific pronunciation
- Sustained shots where scale needs thought; avoid fictional urgent alerts

## Text, data and overlay rules
- Observed / modeled / artist impression flags
- Units AU/km/light years and observation date

## Allowed narrative formats (requires format-pack validation)
`LONG_DOCUMENTARY`, `SHORT_EXPLAINER`, `STEP_LESSON`, `BREAKING_BRIEF`

## Payoff and ending
Explain discovery significance and future measurements.

## Mandatory exception and accuracy gates
- No unlabeled CGI as telescope image
- Agency media reuse licenses are item-specific

## Local compute mode
**CPU without dedicated GPU:** Orbit/path SVG simulation, axis diagrams, text/map motion.
**Optional future GPU:** Later realistic labeled artist impressions with physics checks.

## Golden fixture and independent manual acceptance
Catch unit conversion error and simulation presented as observation.

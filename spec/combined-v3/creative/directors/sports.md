# SPORTS — Expert Creative Director v3.0

**Binding:** `domain_id=sports`; implement as **policy + specialist domain algorithms**, never a duplicate renderer. `core/21_EVIDENCE_TO_SCREEN_V3.md` and strict `contracts/v3` govern shared artifacts. **Status: SPECIFIED, NOT IMPLEMENTED.**

## Audience and unique creative objective
Fans seeking fast context, tactics, analysis and honest entertainment across match-specific seasons; distinguish casual vs expert viewers.

## Source/evidence prerequisites
Official fixture/score record plus original context, play-by-play data where licensed, injury updates and applicable sport rules. Avoid treating rumor as match evidence.

## Honest opening (not boilerplate)
Start from a verified decisive question about a game state or coaching choice. Never invent a player quote or claim that the clip proves a hidden motive.

## Story sequence / exact decision policy
1. SHOW verified situation (teams, clock/over, score and match version).
2. DRAW playing area/formation in consistent orientation; identify actual decision.
3. COMPARE what the tactic changes with evidence or rule-based simulation.
4. REVEAL observed outcome separately from plausible alternative.
5. PAY OFF the opening question with clear tactical lesson.

## Approved screen grammar
- Original pitch/gridiron/diamond/court diagrams with actual sport-specific dimensions/terms
- Verified scoreboard cards with source time and timezone
- Player-route arrows / field placements only when sourced or explicitly hypothetical
- Comparison cards for matchup strengths, no broadcast footage by default

## Voice/audio policy
- Hindi/Hinglish commentary with verified cricket names; US English NFL/MLB terminology
- Energy may rise during a documented decisive play, but no fabricated crowd audio; keep score readouts intelligible

## Text, data and overlay rules
- Score/clock/overs states visible with units and source timestamp
- One highlighted tactical route/region at a time; captions never obscure score

## Allowed narrative formats (requires format-pack validation)
`SHORT_EXPLAINER`, `TACTICAL_ANALYSIS`, `BREAKING_BRIEF`, `LONG_DOCUMENTARY`, `REACTION_REVIEW`

## Payoff and ending
One measured tactical takeaway and uncertainty; optional verified follow-up match, never guaranteed result.

## Mandatory exception and accuracy gates
- Broadcast footage and player likeness rights are not automatic
- Do not confuse 17.3 overs with decimal 17.3; verify cricket deliveries
- NFL clock/downs, MLB innings and team-roster corrections are versioned

## Local compute mode
**CPU without dedicated GPU:** SVG field animation, sourced stats, animated arrows, licensed stills, score timeline and own narration.
**Optional future GPU:** Optional generated generic stadium atmosphere labeled illustration; never fake actual play footage.

## Golden fixture and independent manual acceptance
Cricket: 17.3 overs = 105 valid balls; NFL: possession/time-out/down correct, source updates invalidate the diagram.

A human reviewer must inspect actual rendered Short and long-form from this director for factual/rights accuracy, sound, screen readability, novelty, pacing, start/end payoff.

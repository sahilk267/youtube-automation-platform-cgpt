# FORMAT — LONG_DOCUMENTARY (v3.0)

**Purpose:** Narrative evidence-rich investigation or explanatory arc, split into substantive chapters.

**Narrative logic:** Opening promise -> factual context -> multiple evidence chapters -> strongest challenge/alternate explanation -> sourced synthesis -> ending payoff.

**Editing and asset rules:** Allow sustained archival/diagram hold for comprehension, sources visible, music does not outrun truth.

**Duration and delivery:** Choose time using content complexity and observed audience context, not mandated 2-second cut cadence. Shorts and long-form require distinct aspect profiles, composition and meaningful content; no promise that arbitrary runtime boosts reach.

**Audio/text:** Narration must match action or screenshot. Subtitles and semantic evidence overlays are separate; render with native-language fonts. Background sound must have rights and should duck under dialogue. Ending must resolve the opening promise.

**Quality and fail state:** Inspect citation legibility, chapter flow, counterevidence and owner watch-through. Missing rights/source/actual demonstration -> `BLOCKED`. Final viewing/approval is required for representative video. Format rules never override domain factual rules or source permissions.

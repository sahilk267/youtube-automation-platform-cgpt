# FORMAT — SHORT_EXPLAINER (v3.0)

**Purpose:** One factual question, one meaningful explanation and a closed payoff. Short is a distribution size, not a license for thin substance.

**Narrative logic:** Open with visual proof or a truthful viewer question; then provide minimum context, relevant demonstration, one counterpoint if material, concise payoff.

**Editing and asset rules:** Avoid logo-first intros, arbitrary fast cuts or recycled same one-line fact videos.

**Duration and delivery:** Choose time using content complexity and observed audience context, not mandated 2-second cut cadence. Shorts and long-form require distinct aspect profiles, composition and meaningful content; no promise that arbitrary runtime boosts reach.

**Audio/text:** Narration must match action or screenshot. Subtitles and semantic evidence overlays are separate; render with native-language fonts. Background sound must have rights and should duck under dialogue. Ending must resolve the opening promise.

**Quality and fail state:** Check readable vertical captions, visually supported claim and distinct audience value. Missing rights/source/actual demonstration -> `BLOCKED`. Final viewing/approval is required for representative video. Format rules never override domain factual rules or source permissions.

# Business OS — FILING & FINANCIAL RECONCILIATION

1. Retrieve verified legal company identity, accounting period, annual reports/regulatory filings, reporting currency, audit status and amendments.
2. Normalize line items with period/type (quarter/TTM/annual), currency and units; reconcile statements; maintain source-page citations. Never sum incompatible fiscal periods or assume currency conversion date.
3. Compute revenue/margins/cash flow/unit economics with executable deterministic arithmetic and transparent formula sheet; flag missing notes or segment disclosures.
4. Map ownership, beneficial controls, filings and governance events as **documented relationships**, not unsupported influence.
5. Compare competitors using explicitly comparable units and reporting conventions; scenario forecasts labeled assumptions, not facts.
6. Adversarial review challenges survivorship bias, omitted liabilities, undisclosed incentives, accounting policy changes and weak causality.
7. Outputs: reconciled model, citations, assumptions, sensitivity tables and editorial caveats; human review on consequential fraud allegations.

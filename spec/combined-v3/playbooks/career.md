# Career OS — REGION-AWARE OPPORTUNITY RESEARCH

1. Gather employer postings and official occupation/labor data where permitted, with jurisdiction, date, remote/on-site and sample quality.
2. Normalize salary currencies, ranges, experience, geography and date; avoid fabricated placement/salary promises.
3. Map skills, portfolio project requirements, relevant credentials and verification URLs; identify expensive/unnecessary qualifications.
4. Build actionable roadmap with trade-offs, alternative paths and interview practice clearly marked simulation, not employment guarantee.
5. Outputs: market snapshot, verified credentials, skills dependencies, sample caveats, project plan and user-safety review.

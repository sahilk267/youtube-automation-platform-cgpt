# Culture OS — MEME ORIGIN, CONTEXT AND DIFFUSION

1. Search earliest *observed* version of trend/meme with timestamped archive and platform evidence; explicitly avoid claiming universal first origin from incomplete datasets.
2. Track spread across communities/regions/languages, noting accessible sample, deletion gaps and algorithmic visibility biases.
3. Examine cultural and linguistic context with independent sources; rumor/satire and parody need separate status.
4. Enforce creator attribution, clip/audio rights, privacy and non-harassment. Never identify a private person by online speculation.
5. Produce original explanatory diagrams and narrative that adds understanding beyond reposting the trend.
6. Outputs: tentative origin report, diffusion timeline, context alternatives, permission notes and original scene plan.

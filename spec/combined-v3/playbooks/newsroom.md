# Newsroom OS — FORENSIC SPEECH/CLAIM/INFLUENCE CHAIN

1. Trigger collection preserves original clip URL and date; retrieve **full original speech** or explicitly block quote certainty if unavailable. Identify speaker/date/location and transcript source. Clip/crop flags are uncertainty indicators, not automatic evidence of deception.
2. Segment audio into time-coded language spans; keep raw-language transcript and separately versioned translations with confidence; names require entity disambiguation and human review for major allegations.
3. Extract atomic promises, observations, claims, predictions, opinions and rhetorical statements. Evidence collector retrieves earlier statements by topic, synonyms, multilingual spellings, archive date and primary record.
4. Contradiction engine compares same proposition including timeframe, scope, quantifier, condition and changed circumstances; outputs CONSISTENT / UPDATED_POSITION / APPARENT_CONTRADICTION / CONFIRMED_CONTRADICTION / UNVERIFIABLE / OUT_OF_CONTEXT with actual supporting and opposing passages. Do not classify entire person as liar.
5. Investigate policy/action/outcome using statutes, budgets, official orders, statements, court records and independently sourced statistics. Ownership/funding/media network edges need documented dates, relationship type and alternative explanations. Correlation doesn't prove collusion or control.
6. Competing interpretations must be included in proportion to evidence quality, not forced partisan symmetry. Serious accusations require right-of-reply tracking and legal/editorial review before publication. Comments/social media are leads, never independently factual proof.
7. Render original timelines, quote cards with timestamp and labeled reenactments; synthetic footage cannot masquerade as a real event. Source corrections propagate into old case files and pending scripts.
8. Halt when full speech unavailable for decisive context, primary source blocked, response request unresolved at legally significant stage, or factual claim confidence insufficient; issue explicit public caveats or withhold.

**Metrics:** false-positive contradiction rate, source provenance coverage, response logs, correction speed and contextual fairness.

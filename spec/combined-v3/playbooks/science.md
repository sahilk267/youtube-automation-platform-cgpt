# Science OS — EVIDENCE & REPRODUCIBILITY CHAIN

1. Discover published paper/preprint with exact DOI/version, funding/conflicts, date and retraction/correction status. Distinguish preprint from peer-reviewed work.
2. Examine study design, n, control groups, sampling, confounders, measurement methods, effect size, uncertainty and limitations. If the primary paper is inaccessible, do not claim methods were checked.
3. Recalculate reported statistics when raw inputs permit; log formula, units, notebook hash and sensitivity assumptions. Never transform relative risk to absolute risk without denominator.
4. Compare replication attempts, meta-analyses and field consensus; cite and distinguish scholarly disagreement vs established results. Retraction/erratum invalidates dependent claims.
5. Mechanism explainer and visualizer produce original diagrams; simulations carry assumption labels and are not presented as observations.
6. Adversarial critique probes alternate hypotheses, p-hacking risk, inappropriate causal conclusions, generalization and recent contrary studies.
7. Outputs: paper provenance bundle, methods checklist, calculation evidence, consensus/uncertainty map, educational scene plan. High-impact health interpretations require cautious human review.

# FORMAT — STEP_LESSON (v3.0)

**Purpose:** Measurable learner outcome with prerequisites and genuine practice.

**Narrative logic:** Objective -> prerequisite -> worked step -> misconception -> guided exercise -> answer.

**Editing and asset rules:** Not every word requires a cut; keep equations and examples on screen for thinking time.

**Duration and delivery:** Choose time using content complexity and observed audience context, not mandated 2-second cut cadence. Shorts and long-form require distinct aspect profiles, composition and meaningful content; no promise that arbitrary runtime boosts reach.

**Audio/text:** Narration must match action or screenshot. Subtitles and semantic evidence overlays are separate; render with native-language fonts. Background sound must have rights and should duck under dialogue. Ending must resolve the opening promise.

**Quality and fail state:** Correctness, accessibility, visual contrast, and manual learner walkthrough. Missing rights/source/actual demonstration -> `BLOCKED`. Final viewing/approval is required for representative video. Format rules never override domain factual rules or source permissions.
